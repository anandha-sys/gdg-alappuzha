About Our Chapter

Google Developer Group (GDG) Alappuzha is a diverse community of developers, tech enthusiasts, and innovators. Whether you are building scalable cloud architectures or writing your very first lines of code from the serene stretches of Kuttanad, our goal is to foster learning, networking, and growth. We host workshops, hackathons, and speaker sessions centered around Google technologies and beyond.
